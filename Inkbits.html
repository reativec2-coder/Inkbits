by Tharun

Tap a digit and it flips along with the four digits touching it. Clear every 1 before you run out of taps.

You get a limited number of taps: the minimum needed plus a small cushion that shrinks as levels rise. Finish at the minimum for 3 stars, within 2 extra taps for 2, otherwise 1. Hints show the best next tap but count as normal taps.

How to play

→
  1. Goal: turn every 1 on the board into a 0.
  2. Tap a digit: it flips, and so do the digits directly above, below, left and right of it. A 1 becomes 0 and a 0 becomes 1.
  3. Watch your taps: you only get a limited number. Fewer taps earn more stars.
  4. Stuck? Hint points to a good next tap, and Undo takes a tap back.
